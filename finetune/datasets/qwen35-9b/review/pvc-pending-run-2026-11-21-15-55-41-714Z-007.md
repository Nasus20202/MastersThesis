## Root Cause Analysis

## Fix Applied

1. **Deleted the unbound PVC** - Since the PVC never bound, there was no data to preserve. Used force deletion.
2. **Recreated the PVC** with identical specifications except changing `storageClassName` from `fast-ssd` to `standard`.
