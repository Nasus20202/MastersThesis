## Final report

Fixed and verified.

**Cause:** The Deployment `app` was updated to `readOnlyRootFilesystem: true`, but the nginx image must write to `/var/cache/nginx` (temp dirs) and `/var/run` (pid file). The new pod crashed at startup with `mkdir() "/var/cache/nginx/client_temp" failed (30: Read-only file system)`, never passed its TCP readiness probe, and the rollout stalled (`1 updatedReplicas`, `1 unavailableReplicas`, new RS at 1/0 ready).

**Change (smallest, owner-level fix on Deployment `app`):** kept `readOnlyRootFilesystem: true` and added two `emptyDir` volumes with mounts only at nginx's writable paths:

- `nginx-cache` → `/var/cache/nginx`
- `nginx-run` → `/var/run` (resolves to `/run` in this image)
