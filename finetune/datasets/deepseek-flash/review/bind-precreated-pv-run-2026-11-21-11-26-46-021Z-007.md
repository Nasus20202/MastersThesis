## What I found

- `app-pv` was pre-created and `Available`: 1Gi, `ReadWriteOnce`, `storageClassName: manual`, `hostPath: /tmp/app-pv`, reclaim policy `Retain`.
- The cluster's **default** StorageClass is `standard` (rancher.io/local-path, `WaitForFirstConsumer`). A PVC created without an explicit class would have taken `standard` and dynamically provisioned a _new_ volume instead of binding `app-pv` — that was the key constraint to respect.

## Change made

Applied one manifest (`/tmp/work/app.yaml`):

- **PVC `app-pvc`**: `storageClassName: manual` (so it matches the PV, not the default class), `volumeName: app-pv` (explicit binding), `ReadWriteOnce`, request `1Gi` — exactly matching the PV's mode/capacity, so no provisioning and no resizing of the PV.
- **Pod `app`**: `image: nginx:1.31.5`, volume `appdata` → `persistentVolumeClaim.claimName: app-pvc`, mounted at `mountPath: /data`.

The PV's own spec was left untouched (capacity, class, reclaim policy, hostPath all unchanged), and no other cluster objects were modified.
